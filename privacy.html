Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Four in a Row Gauntlet ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what limited information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration and do not collect, store, or sell your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. Online Arena & Temporary Match Data

If you choose to use the optional Online Arena feature, you may enter a temporary player nickname and join or create a match room. This temporary session data (nickname, room code, and board moves) is transmitted via Google Firebase solely to synchronize live gameplay and is automatically deleted within 45 seconds after a session closes.

3. Local Storage

Game settings, local match statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase Realtime Database strictly for temporary online multiplayer matchmaking and does not use third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the support contact listed on our Google Play Store page.
